Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Padawan Ops is a private, single-user application. Its only user is the owner of the Google account that authorises it.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission it reads the owner's own Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Drive, and creates calendar events and labels emails when the owner asks it to.

How the data is used

The data is used only to help the owner manage their own household administration (bills, receipts and family events). It is stored on a private server controlled by the owner.

Sharing

Data from Google accounts is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train general-purpose AI models. Padawan Ops's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
